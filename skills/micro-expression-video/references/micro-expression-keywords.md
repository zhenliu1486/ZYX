## 基础情绪

### 1. 开心/喜悦
- **眼睛**：crinkling eyes, smiling eyes, eye corners lifting, squinting slightly
- **嘴部**：genuine smile, corners of mouth lifting, showing teeth, laughing
- **面部**：cheeks lifting, natural blush
- **完整关键词**：`genuine happiness, smiling eyes, warm smile, cheeks lifting, natural laughter`

### 2. 悲伤/难过
- **眼睛**：teary eyes, welling up, glassy eyes, gaze lowering, red-rimmed eyes
- **嘴部**：trembling lips, chin quivering, pressing lips together to hold back tears
- **面部**：pale face, downturned mouth, eyebrows drawing together
- **完整关键词**：`sadness, teary eyes, trembling lips, holding back tears, pale face, downturned expression`

### 3. 愤怒
- **眼睛**：narrowed eyes, glaring, sharp gaze, eyebrows furrowing
- **嘴部**：clenched jaw, thin lips, teeth grinding, nostril flaring
- **面部**：red face, tense muscles, veins on forehead
- **完整关键词**：`anger, narrowed glaring eyes, clenched jaw, tense facial muscles, red complexion`

### 4. 惊讶
- **眼睛**：widening eyes, raised eyebrows, pupils dilating, staring
- **嘴部**：mouth falling open, lips parting slightly
- **面部**：frozen expression, stillness
- **完整关键词**：`surprise, wide eyes, raised eyebrows, slightly open mouth, frozen still expression`

### 5. 恐惧
- **眼睛**：wide panicked eyes, darting gaze, pale face
- **嘴部**：dry lips, swallowing hard, mouth slightly open
- **面部**：sweating, pale, tense
- **完整关键词**：`fear, panicked wide eyes, pale face, sweating, tense jaw, nervous swallowing`

### 6. 厌恶/嫌弃
- **眼睛**：sidelong glance, judging look, looking down nose
- **嘴部**：upper lip curling, nose wrinkling
- **面部**：distasteful expression, chin lifting
- **完整关键词**：`disgust, upper lip curl, nose wrinkle, sidelong judgmental glance, chin lifting`

## 复杂情绪

### 1. 忍住不哭
```
holding back tears, trembling lower lip, blinking rapidly to keep tears in,
eyes glistening, chin quivering, nostrils flaring slightly, jaw tight
```

### 2. 强忍怒火
```
holding back anger, clenched jaw, tight lips, nostrils flaring,
eyes narrowing slightly, breathing through nose, controlling expression
```

### 3. 欲言又止
```
hesitant expression, lips parting then closing, eyes darting away,
swallowing hard, looking down then up, uncertain gaze
```

### 4. 苦笑/无奈
```
bitter smile, sad half-smile, corners of mouth twitching,
eyes looking away, slight head shake, weary expression
```

### 5. 害羞/不好意思
```
shy expression, eyes darting down, cheeks flushing pink,
small smile, biting lower lip, fidgeting, avoiding eye contact
```

### 6. 魅惑/挑逗
```
seductive half-lidded eyes, slow blink, slight smirk,
lips parting, head tilting, gaze holding, playful expression
```

### 7. 冷漠/无所谓
```
blank expression, deadpan eyes, neutral mouth,
unmoving features, dismissive glance, slight eye roll
```

### 8. 温柔/深情
```
soft warm eyes, gentle smile, gaze lingering,
eyebrows softening, calm expression, loving look
```

### 9. 震惊/不敢相信
```
shocked disbelief, wide eyes, mouth slightly open,
head shaking slightly, hand half-raised, frozen expression
```

### 10. 委屈
```
wronged expression, teary eyes, quivering lip,
looking down, eyebrows downturned, hurt gaze
```

## 微动作细节

### 眼部微动作
- **眨眼频率**：natural blinking every 3-5 seconds
- **眼神移动**：subtle eye movements, glancing sideways, looking away
- **瞳孔**：pupils dilating/contracting slightly
- **眼泪**：tears welling up, single tear rolling down, glistening eyes

### 嘴部微动作
- **吞咽**：swallowing hard, throat moving
- **抿嘴**：pressing lips together, thin lips
- **咬唇**：biting lower lip, nervous habit
- **嘴角抽动**：corners of mouth twitching, micro-smile

### 头部微动作
- **点头**：slight nodding, subtle head movement
- **歪头**：head tilting slightly, curious/listening
- **低头**：looking down, chin lowering
- **仰头**：chin lifting, proud/determined

### 呼吸/颈部
- **呼吸起伏**：subtle breathing motion, chest rising
- **吞咽**：throat bobbing, swallowing
- **颈部肌肉**：neck tendons showing, tense neck

### 皮肤细节
- **出汗**：sweat on forehead, slight perspiration
- **脸红**：blushing cheeks, flushed face
- **苍白**：pale complexion, blood draining from face
- **皮肤纹理**：skin texture, pores, natural skin lines
