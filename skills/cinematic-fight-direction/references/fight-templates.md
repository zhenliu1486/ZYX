## 武侠对决模板

**适用场景**：古风、仙侠、江湖，两人对决。总时长约30秒，8个镜头。

### 分镜表

| 镜号 | 时长 | 景别 | 动作 | 运镜 | 提示词 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3s | 全景 | 两人对峙站立，风吹衣袂 | 固定，缓慢推近 | `wide shot, two warriors facing each other, wind blowing robes, ancient courtyard, slow push in, cinematic, wuxia` |
| 2 | 2s | 特写 | 一方眼神变冷，手握剑柄 | 固定 | `extreme close-up, cold eyes, hand gripping sword hilt, dramatic lighting` |
| 3 | 2s | 中景 | 一方拔剑出鞘，剑光一闪 | 快速横摇 | `medium shot, drawing sword, flash of blade, quick pan, dynamic motion` |
| 4 | 1s | 近景 | 两人快速交手三招 | 手持快切 | `close shot, quick martial arts exchange, fast cuts, motion blur, intense` |
| 5 | 3s | 中近景 | 一招对掌，气浪迸发 | 慢动作，环绕 | `medium close-up, palms clashing, energy blast, slow motion, orbit shot, dust explosion` |
| 6 | 2s | 特写 | 一方嘴角流血，震惊 | 固定 | `close-up, blood at corner of mouth, shocked expression, sweat, dramatic` |
| 7 | 3s | 全景 | 一方后退落地，另一方持剑而立 | 缓慢拉远 | `wide shot, one stepping back landing, other standing with sword, pull back, epic` |
| 8 | 4s | 远景 | 两人分立两侧，落叶飘下 | 固定，渐慢 | `long shot, two figures apart, falling leaves, wind, melancholic, cinematic` |

### 变体：仙侠斗法
- 将拔剑换成：`flying sword, magic energy, spells, glowing runes`
- 将对掌换成：`energy blast, magical collision, shockwave`
