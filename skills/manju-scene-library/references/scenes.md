## 都市现代

### 室内

#### 总裁办公室
- **基础**：`luxurious corner office, floor-to-ceiling windows, city skyline, mahogany desk, leather executive chair, bookshelf, minimal luxury style`
- **日景**：morning sunlight through windows, bright natural light, clean shadows
- **夜景**：night city lights, warm desk lamp, cool blue moonlight, low key
- **情绪-霸气**：backlight silhouette, dramatic rim light, high contrast
- **情绪-疲惫**：dim desk lamp, scattered papers, tired atmosphere

#### 现代公寓/家
- **基础**：`modern minimalist apartment, open living room, kitchen island, large sofa, floor-to-ceiling curtains, warm wood floors`
- **日景**：natural light, bright and airy, cozy
- **夜景**：city lights through window, warm lamp glow, intimate atmosphere
- **雨天**：rain on window, soft indoor light, melancholic mood

#### 酒店套房
- **基础**：`luxury hotel suite, king bed, white linens, city view windows, marble bathroom, golden lighting`
- **夜景**：warm lamps, dark curtains, romantic
- **清晨**：morning light through curtains, soft and hazy

#### 餐厅/高级餐厅
- **基础**：`fine dining restaurant, dim ambient light, candlelight, white tablecloth, wine glasses, elegant interior`
- **约会**：candlelight, soft glow, intimate
- **谈判**：sharp lighting, serious atmosphere, private room

#### 医院病房
- **基础**：`hospital room, white walls, medical equipment, bed window, sterile lighting`
- **白天**：cold fluorescent light, clinical
- **夜晚**：dim night light, quiet, blue tone

### 室外

#### 都市街道
- **基础**：`city street, tall buildings, traffic, pedestrians, crosswalk`
- **清晨**：empty street, morning light, fresh
- **黄昏**：golden hour, traffic lights, warm orange
- **深夜**：neon lights, wet asphalt reflection, few people
- **雨天**：rain, umbrella, neon reflection, cinematic

#### 天台
- **基础**：`rooftop, city skyline, night sky, edge railing, urban view`
- **夜景**：city lights below, starry sky, wind
- **黄昏**：sunset sky, orange and purple

#### 停车场
- **基础**：`underground parking garage, concrete pillars, fluorescent lights, cars, echoey`
- **紧张**：dim corner, single light source, shadows
- **追逐**：motion blur, dark corners, suspense

#### 酒吧/夜店
- **基础**：`luxury bar, dark interior, neon lights, bottles shelf, bar counter, lounge music vibe`
- **氛围**：neon blue and purple, silhouettes, smoky

### 室外

- **基础**：`chinese mountain landscape, misty peaks, pine trees, waterfall, ancient pavilion, wuxia scenery`
- **晨雾**：sea of clouds, soft morning light, ethereal xianxia
- **日落**：golden mountain, warm light, peaceful
- **雪景**：snow on pines, white mountains, cold and pure

#### 城楼/城墙
- **基础**：`ancient chinese city wall, battlements, watchtower, sunset over city`

- **决战**：storm clouds, dramatic, epic

#### 集市/街道
- **基础**：`ancient chinese market street, lanterns, shops, crowds, vendors, wooden buildings`

- **夜市**：lanterns glow, bustling, festive

#### 渡口/湖边
