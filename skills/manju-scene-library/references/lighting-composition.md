## 构图术语速查

| 中文 | 英文关键词 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 全景 | wide shot, full shot | 交代环境 |
| 中景 | medium shot | 对话、动作 |
| 近景/半身 | close shot, medium close-up | 表情+手势 |
| 特写 | close-up | 情绪重点 |
| 大特写 | extreme close-up | 眼睛/细节 |
| 俯拍 | high angle, top-down | 渺小、孤独 |
| 仰拍 | low angle, worm's eye view | 强势、威严 |
| 平视 | eye level, neutral | 客观、自然 |
| 过肩 | over-the-shoulder | 对话镜头 |
| 跟随 | tracking shot, follow shot | 运动感 |
| 推镜 | dolly in, push in | 强调、发现 |
| 拉镜 | dolly out, pull out | 疏离、结束 |

### 构图法则
```
rule of thirds, golden ratio, leading lines, symmetrical composition,
negative space, depth of field, foreground/midground/background layers
```

## 光影类型

### 1. 自然光
- **黄金小时（日出后/日落前1小时）**：`golden hour, warm sunlight, long shadows, soft and flattering`
- **蓝调时刻（日出前/日落后）**：`blue hour, cool blue tones, city lights starting, magical`
- **正午硬光**：`harsh midday sun, strong shadows, high contrast`
- **阴天柔光**：`overcast sky, soft diffused light, even illumination`

### 2. 人造光
- **烛光/暖灯**：`candlelight, warm glow, intimate, flickering shadows`
- **霓虹**：`neon lights, blue and pink, wet street reflection, cyberpunk vibe`
- **聚光灯**：`spotlight, single source, dark surroundings, dramatic`
- **窗光**：`window light, soft side light, Rembrandt lighting, classic portrait`

### 3. 特效光
- **丁达尔光/耶稣光**：`volumetric light, god rays, light beams through mist/trees`
- **逆光/轮廓光**：`backlight, rim light, silhouette, glowing edges`
- **闪烁/动态光**：`flickering light, dancing shadows, firelight`

## 电影感参数包

### 通用电影感后缀（所有镜头必加）
```
cinematic, 8K, photorealistic, film grain, 35mm,
professional color grading, depth of field,
masterpiece, best quality
```

### 胶片质感变体
- **35mm胶片**：`35mm film, kodak portra 400, film grain, natural colors`
- **黑白电影**：`black and white, film noir, high contrast, monochrome`
- **王家卫风格**：`wong kar wai style, neon, slow motion, moody, step printing`
- **韦斯安德森**：`wes anderson style, symmetrical, pastel colors, centered composition`

### 负面词（防AI感）
```
CGI, 3D render, anime, cartoon, illustration, painting,
over-saturated, HDR, watermark, text, signature,
deformed, blurry, low quality, pixelated
```

## 按画幅调整

### 9:16（竖屏短剧/漫剧）
- **构图**：人物居中偏上，留出下方字幕空间
- **关键词**：`vertical composition, 9:16, phone screen, full body or half body`
- **适合**：人物特写、情绪镜头、竖屏分镜

### 16:9（横屏电影感）
- **构图**：宽幅展现环境，人物在三分线
- **关键词**：`widescreen, 16:9, cinematic, panoramic`
- **适合**：大场景、动作戏、双人对话

### 3:4（小红书/封面）
- **构图**：人物半身，重点突出面部表情
- **关键词**：`portrait orientation, 3:4, cover style, eye-catching`

### 1:1（方图/头像）
- **构图**：面部居中，紧凑
- **关键词**：`square, 1:1, profile picture, centered face`
